# About & Details
#### Standard Mode
> Familiar normal calculator for most calculations. Simple and easy to use.
#### Scientifc Mode
> Supports a full suite of functions including trigonometry (sin, cos, tan, and their inverses), logarithms (log₁₀, ln, logᵦ), roots (square and cube), and powers.
#### History Tape
> A dedicated panel that tracks all past calculations with timestamps, allowing you to clear or recall them.
#### High Precision Engine
> A specialized precision mode that utilizes a high-precision arithmetic engine to handle complex decimal calculations accurately.
## Symbolic Algebra & Equations
> Symbolic CAS: Capability to simplify, expand, and evaluate algebraic expressions using variables like x, y, and z.
#### Equation Solver
> Simple algebraic solver <br>
> Specialized tools for polynomial division and simplifying rational expressions
## Calculus & Differential Equations
#### Derivatives
> Supports both symbolic differentiation (up to the 4th order) and numerical differentiation at a specific point.
#### Numerical Integration
> Includes tools for definite and indefinite integration using numerical methods.
#### Differential Equations (ODEs)
> A dedicated section for solving ordinary differential equations.
