## About & Details
#### Standard Mode
> Familiar normal calculator for most calculations. Simple and easy to use.
#### Scientifc Mode
> Supports a full suite of functions including trigonometry (sin, cos, tan, and their inverses), logarithms (log₁₀, ln, logᵦ), roots (square and cube), and powers.
#### History Tape
> A dedicated panel that tracks all past calculations with timestamps, allowing you to clear or recall them.
#### High Precision Engine
> A specialized precision mode that utilizes a high-precision arithmetic engine to handle complex decimal calculations accurately.
